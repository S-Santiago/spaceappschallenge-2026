Why we suggest it
  • Pea: rain December to May averages 293 mm, plus about 69 mm stored in the soil, for a water need of 350 to 500 mm.

Is FarmLens a weather forecast?

No. It counts what happened in 21 years of NASA data for the area around your field, so you can plan a rotation for a normal year.

How exact is FarmLens for my plot?

Climate values describe an area of about 50 km and greenness a 250 m square. Valleys, slopes and the coast can differ.

Does FarmLens tell me which crop to plant?

No. It compares four rotations and lists what to check with a local adviser. You decide.

Does the app work without coverage?

The three demo fields open without a connection after the first visit. A new point on the map needs one.

Is the app in Spanish?

Not yet. This first version is in English; a Spanish version is the next step.

What does the app cost?

Nothing. It opens in the phone's browser, and the code is open under the MIT licence.
